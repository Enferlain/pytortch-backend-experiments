# Inductor backend contract coverage

Generated by `python -m cubecl_torch.testing.coverage_inventory`; do not edit by hand.

Every op of Inductor's `OpsHandler`, every `ReductionType` and every `BackendFeature` in the pinned PyTorch, plus surfaces Inductor does not enumerate, assigned to a family with its status. Families list their prerequisites; that order is the work order, tracked in beads.

Statuses:

- **implemented**: works for the declared domain in its note (dtypes, modes, exclusions); says nothing about the rest of Inductor's contract for the item.
- **complete**: every dtype, mode and argument form Inductor can request for the item, under the numerical policy (family policy), with tests; none yet.
- **missing**: rejected by the capability checks.
- **n/a**: not applicable to this backend.
